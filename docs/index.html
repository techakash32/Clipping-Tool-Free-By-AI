What is it?

Clipping Tool Free By AI is a free, self-hosted AI video clipper. Paste a YouTube link and it downloads and transcribes the video, scores the most clip-worthy moments, cuts them to 9:16 with face tracking and animated captions, checks quality, then writes titles and hashtags and posts to YouTube Shorts.

GTA 6 (GTA VI) clipping

Clip GTA 6 trailer breakdowns, news, leak coverage, theory podcasts and gameplay you have permission to use. Includes a built-in GTA 6 hashtag pack.

Quick start

git clone https://github.com/techakash32/Clipping-Tool-Free-By-AI.git
Run install.bat (Windows) or bash install.sh, then python webapp/server.py and open http://127.0.0.1:8787.
